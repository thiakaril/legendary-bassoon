# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Nuxt 4.6 with pnpm, already scaffolded in this repository. The marketing surface lives in `app/` and ships as a static-first Nuxt page.

## Users

Primary: HR and operations leads at Indonesian small and mid-market companies, roughly 20–500 employees, who run people operations across spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads, and disconnected payroll or attendance tools.

Secondary: founders and operations leaders comparing HR software before they hire a dedicated HR person.

The page serves both English- and Indonesian-speaking visitors.

## Product Purpose

Hruno is an AI-native HR platform that brings employee records, leave, attendance, payroll, and recruitment into one place. An AI copilot answers questions and carries out routine HR work across that data. Success for this surface is a qualified waitlist signup or a booked product demo before public launch.

## Positioning

Hruno has the breadth of an HR suite like Horilla, but the AI is part of the system of record rather than a bolted-on chatbot: the copilot can read and act across people, leave, attendance, payroll, and hiring with permission-aware access. This is the working position for the pre-launch page and should be confirmed as modules ship.

## Operating Context

HR admin happens daily; payroll runs monthly; leave requests, attendance exceptions, and hiring pipelines move continuously. Indonesian employment context matters, but no compliance certification or statutory coverage is confirmed. The audience reads on phones between meetings and on desktop during evaluation. Bilingual English and Indonesian is a product expectation, not a marketing nicety.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed: web SaaS; core HR suite plus AI copilot; pre-launch; waitlist and demo booking are the only real actions.

Undecided and not to be invented: exact module list, integrations, pricing, customer count, benchmarks, security certifications, statutory compliance claims, and launch date.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Hruno. Domain: hruno.ai.id. Bilingual English and Indonesian.

No logo, palette, typeface, photography, or product screenshots were supplied. A typographic wordmark and authored synthetic interface demonstrations are acceptable for this pre-launch surface, and must be labeled as synthetic where a visitor could mistake them for live product data.

## Evidence on Hand

No customers, testimonials, case studies, press, product screenshots, pricing, or performance data exist yet. Those claims must not be fabricated.

The only real actions are joining the waitlist and requesting a demo. Both currently resolve to an email to `hello@hruno.ai.id` with a prefilled subject and body; this must be replaced with a real form endpoint before launch.

## Product Principles

1. The copilot is the brain of the system of record, not a chat ornament.
2. One place for the unglamorous work: leave, attendance, payroll, and hiring.
3. Built for Indonesian teams, bilingual by default.
4. Calm, legible, and trustworthy; no HR jargon and no hype.
5. Show the product doing the work instead of claiming magic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, and a genuine Indonesian translation rather than machine-translated marketing copy.
