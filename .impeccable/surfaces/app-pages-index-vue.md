# Surface brief — hruno.ai.id landing

Mode: Persuade. Scope: first public marketing surface for a pre-launch product.

## Audience and job

HR and operations leads at Indonesian SMBs (roughly 20–500 people) who run people ops across spreadsheets, WhatsApp and disconnected payroll tools. They need to understand, in one screen, that Hruno covers the whole HR record and that its AI copilot acts on that record rather than chatting beside it.

## Action

Primary: join the waitlist. Secondary: book a demo. Both resolve to a prefilled email to hello@hruno.ai.id until a form endpoint exists. No fake trial, login or payment.

## Content and constraints

No customers, testimonials, logos, pricing, benchmarks or compliance certifications exist. Product views are authored synthetic demonstrations and must be labelled. Two credited architectural photographs carry the atmosphere; the rest of the imagery is real HTML interface fragments. Bilingual English/Indonesian. WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard and reduced-motion support.

## Chosen direction

The well-kept board. A dark lunar ground of flat tiles with 8px gaps: the HR record as one tidy operations board, the copilot as the hand moving across it. Typographic name band, product UI fragments at true size, bronze used only on the action and selected states.

Grounded candidates considered: split-flap board, paper ledger, acetate manual, jacquard punch cards, HyperCard stack, Game Boy tile field, well-kept board. The well-kept board was the quietest of the seven and the one that could carry five modules plus a copilot without turning into a dashboard. The split-flap board is competitive on operational clarity; its discipline of live rows that update in place is kept in the records board. The ledger's traceability discipline is kept in the payroll fragment. The jacquard's "every thread choice is traceable" is kept in the row detail. The others are declined.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Hruno is the HR suite as a well-kept board: every record is a tile, and the copilot is the hand that moves across it. The page refuses the centred hero over a generic dashboard screenshot.

OWN-WORLD: Lunar black (#111111) ground, panel (#1c1c1c), ink (#f2f2f2), muted (#8f8f8f), bronze accent (#a08672), bronze ink (#111111). General Sans, three weights. 8px outer margins and gaps. Flat filled tiles, no outlines, no resting shadows, no gradients, square corners. Product interface fragments marked data-ui, built at true size.

STORY: The visitor sees five HR areas in one board, understands that the copilot reads and acts across the same record, believes the admin can be one place, then joins the waitlist or books a demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width name band, "HRUNO" at display size, with a small EN/ID switch. Under it four link tiles. Below, a 2/8/2 rail: left, a credited black-and-white photograph above five facts; centre, the copilot demo tile with a statement band at its foot; right, five flat tiles ending in the bronze action tile.
